# Guideline check

17 November 2026

Eleven society documents were opened and compared with the notes: nine statements were corrected or tightened, the rest agreed. Two documents could not be opened, and five of the nine curriculum critical conditions are not yet covered.

## Checked against society documents

| Topic | Document | Result | Change made |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tricuspid repair at left-sided surgery | [2025 ESC/EACTS valve guideline, summary table](https://rmlg.uliege.be/download/4110/3511/P-Lancellotti_2025_80_10_0.pdf) | Corrected | Severe TR Class I; moderate TR with annulus ≥40 mm IIa; mild TR with dilated annulus now IIb (the sheet had wrongly said IIb in 2021; it was IIa then) |
| Surgical AF ablation and appendage occlusion | [STS 2023 AF guideline, Cleveland Clinic summary](https://consultqd.clevelandclinic.org/takeaways-from-updated-sts-guidelines-for-surgical-treatment-of-atrial-fibrillation) | Corrected | Ablation at non-mitral surgery is Class I (level B) in STS 2023, not only for AVR and CABG; appendage occlusion Class I (level A) |
| Pulmonary vein isolation alone | [STS 2017 AF guideline](https://www.sts.org/sites/default/files/documents/SurgicalTreatmentAfib.pdf) | Added | Not recommended alone with left atrium ≥4.5 cm or more than moderate MR |
| Endocarditis timing and reconstruction | [AATS 2016 endocarditis consensus, ACC summary](https://acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/ten-points-to-remember/2017/06/22/13/10/2016-the-american-association-for-thoracic-surgery) | Tightened | Delay after intracranial haemorrhage is 3 weeks or more in AATS (4 weeks in ESC); biological root preferred for destruction beyond the annulus; avoid mechanical valves after intracranial bleeding |
| Empyema | [AATS 2017 empyema guideline](https://www.aast.org/Assets/0a6bd0b8-32d9-4c58-b44e-e062c78a0189/638659079374000000/t6-aats-guideline-empyema-j-thorac-cardiovasc-surg-2017-pdf) | Difference flagged | AATS uses LDH >1,000 and glucose <2.2 mmol/L, advises against routine fibrinolytics and puts VATS first for stage II; the sheet's table follows BTS and MIST-2 |
| Early-stage lung cancer | [AATS 2023 expert consensus, Guideline Central](https://guidelinecentral.com/guideline/3110985) | Corrected | Nodal standard is three mediastinal stations and one hilar station; neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy preferred for operable stage II–III; no routine radiotherapy for unexpected N2 |
| Pulmonary metastasectomy | [STS 2019 expert consensus](https://core.ac.uk/download/162543658.pdf) | Agreed, detail added | Best with three or fewer metastases; minimally invasive preferred; pneumonectomy discouraged |
| Arrest after cardiac surgery | [STS expert consensus on resuscitation](https://www.sts.org/sites/default/files/documents/ExpertConsensus_ResuscitationAfterCardiacSurgery.pdf) | Agreed, detail added | Three shocks or pacing before massage, amiodarone 300 mg, no routine adrenaline, resternotomy within 5 minutes up to day 10 |
| Tetralogy of Fallot timing | [AATS 2022 expert consensus (abstract)](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/the-american-association-for-thoracic-surgery-aats-2022-expert-co/) | Agreed, detail added | Repair at 3–6 months in asymptomatic infants; palliation or primary repair in symptomatic neonates |
| Aortic root, ascending, arch and heritable disease | [EACTS/STS 2024 aortic guideline, Guideline Central](https://www.guidelinecentral.com/guideline/3522536/) | Agreed | Checked when the main tab was written; no change |

## Could not be checked

- 2024 EACTS perioperative medication guideline: full text not reachable. The drug-stopping table (ticagrelor 3 days, clopidogrel 5, prasugrel 7) matches the 2017 EACTS guideline; the SGLT2 inhibitor and DOAC intervals are unverified.
- 2024 ERAS/STS perioperative care statement: only the abstract opened. The ERACS bundle, glucose and temperature targets are unverified.
- No SCTS clinical guideline was found for these topics. SCTS aligns its courses to the ISCP curriculum, so the curriculum was used as the SCTS benchmark.
- Still from standard references only: nodule pathway (BTS), TNM 9 stage groups, fitness thresholds, thymoma, trauma triggers, mitral and tricuspid technique notes, most of the congenital tab, and the echo grading tables in the main tab.
